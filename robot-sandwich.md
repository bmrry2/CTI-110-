# Robot Sandwich 

## Robot will be making a BLT sandwich. Two slices of toasted bread are on the counter along with 3 pieces of prepped lettuce, 3 slices of cooked turkey bacon, and 2 slices of seasoned lightly with salt and pepper tomato. Mayo is already measured and on a knife as well just ready to slide onto the toasted bread. Robot is standing at counter with everything, including a red plate, laid out in front, it's hands are empty but it does have thumbs. 

### 1. Grab knife with mayo on it 
### 2. Grab one slice of toasted bread
### 3. Spread mayo on one slice of bread on the widest, flat part of the bread
Use a left to right, back and forth sweeping motion to spread mayo
### 4. Set knife back down on counter gently
### 5.Grab lettuce from counter gently
### 6.Place 3 pieces of lettuce on mayo side of toast
### 7. Place 3 slices of tomato on lettuce gently making sure that they overlap and balance on the lettuce
### 8. Place 3 slices of cooked turkey bacon on top of tomato 
Make sure the turkey bacon is balanced on the tomato that is over the lettuce
### 9. Take the other slice of bread and place the widest, flattest part of the bread on the sliced turkey bacon
### 10. Place hands gently on the sandwich and give a gentle press down to ensure everything stays put 
### 11.Use knife to cut sandwich up and down then left to right. We are cutting sandwich into as even as possible squares. 
### 12. Done when sandwich placed on red plate

## ENJOY!
